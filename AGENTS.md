# Project instructions

## Locked public navigation

The navigation is final and must be treated as a locked public component.

- Reuse `components/site-header.js` and `components/site-header.css` on every page.
- Page work may only change the active route; do not alter existing navigation visuals or interactions.
- Do not duplicate, redesign, or rewrite the header for a new page.
- The locked boundary includes the Chinese logo, handwritten W/signature and its confirmed position, supporting signature copy, all three gray lines and their confirmed positions, every Tab state, and sticky behavior.
- Do not change sizes, spacing, colors, type, assets, hover, focus, selected states, lines, signature position, or sticky behavior.
- Add new navigation content without modifying existing items or shared styles.
- If a page cannot reuse the component unchanged, explain why and ask for approval before editing it.
- See `NAVIGATION_LOCK.md` for the integration contract.
